How This App Helps Students (PSC Exam Prep)

Gamified Learning

By earning XP and leveling up, your study becomes an engaging game. This increases motivation and makes tedious revision feel rewarding.

Spaced Repetition System (SRS) & Backlog

The **Revision Plan** uses a scientifically proven Spaced Repetition System. Questions are scheduled for review on specific days (1, 3, 7, 14, 30, 60, 90 days after upload or last mastery) to ensure long-term retention. Crucially, due revisions remain in your **backlog** until you clear them with a successful score (70%+).

Focus & Efficiency

The time-bound practice mode simulates exam conditions, helping you improve speed and accuracy. The **Question Set Management** allows you or an admin to curate highly relevant study material. The full-screen exam mode helps you focus without distractions.
